How to read this

Safe tests

Sentinel imitates the shape of common attacker behavior with harmless local no-op commands. Nothing is stolen, changed or sent anywhere.

Why it matters

A broken detection is silent. This is the only way to know your alarms still ring before a real attacker finds out they don't.

Gap vs blind spot

A gap means we saw the activity but no rule fired. A blind spot means the sensor recorded nothing at all.
