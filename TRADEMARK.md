*Last updated: 2026-10-31. Not legal advice.*

"PTAF" and "PTAF Sentinel" are the names of this open-source project. The maintainers do not claim a
registered trademark in them. Please use the names fairly:

- You may say your tool is "compatible with" or "built on" PTAF.
- If you fork it and make material changes, please give your fork a **different name** so users are not
  misled about what they are running or who supports it.
- Do not suggest that the maintainers endorse, sponsor or are affiliated with you without asking.
